Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

How much does full home interior design cost in Islamabad? +

Costs vary by size, materials and scope. After a brief consultation we share a transparent, itemised BOQ and timeline so there are no surprises.

Do you provide 3D designs before execution? +

Yes — 3D interior visualisation is part of our process, so you can approve layouts, finishes and lighting before any work begins.

Do you design and install kitchens and wardrobes? +

Absolutely. We design, manufacture and install kitchens, wardrobes, vanities, media walls and doors to suit your storage and style needs.

How long does a project take? +

A single-room refresh can take 2–4 weeks; full homes vary by scope. We commit to reliable timelines before we start.

Can you work with my existing furniture? +

Yes — we can design around your current pieces or recommend new options within your budget.
