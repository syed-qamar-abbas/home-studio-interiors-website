# Home Studio Interiors — 6-Month SEO, AEO, GEO & LLMO Blog Calendar

Purpose: publish five high-quality guides per month to grow visibility for Islamabad interior design, kitchens, wardrobes, media walls, vanities, doors and remodeling searches while sending qualified homeowners to WhatsApp quote requests.

Publishing rhythm:

- Week 1: Islamabad/local authority guide
- Week 2: core service guide
- Week 3: materials, cost or comparison guide
- Week 4: practical checklist / mistakes / process guide
- Bonus post: portfolio-style inspiration or conversion-focused guide

Every blog should include:

- One strong answer-first intro for AI Overviews and answer engines
- One clear H1, logical H2/H3 structure and natural internal links
- 4–6 FAQs only when they answer real homeowner questions
- A WhatsApp CTA using “Get a Quote” or “Request a Design Consultation”
- A realistic 16:9 cover image, optimized WebP, descriptive alt text
- Links to the relevant service page, `/process`, `/faq`, and one related blog
- No fake reviews, fake stats, fake awards or keyword stuffing

## Cover image production rules

- Style: photorealistic, premium interior design, warm dark luxury, soft orange accent lighting, Islamabad/Pakistani home context where natural.
- Aspect ratios: 16:9 hero cover, 1200×630 OG crop.
- File naming: use descriptive lowercase names such as `kitchen-countertop-guide-islamabad.webp`.
- Performance: WebP, max 1600 px wide, lazy-loaded below the fold.
- Alt text: describe the image naturally; do not stuff keywords.

## WhatsApp conversion notes

- Use a soft CTA near the middle and a stronger CTA at the end.
- Recommended CTA text: “Send your photos and measurements on WhatsApp for a quote direction.”
- Ask for: location, room photos, approximate dimensions, preferred style, timeline and budget range.
- Keep blog pages educational first; conversion should feel helpful, not aggressive.
